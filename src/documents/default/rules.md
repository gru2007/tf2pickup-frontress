1. Matches are found in the game: press **Find a match** in the main menu. The coordinator forms the teams, picks a server and connects you. This site only shows what happens there.
2. **Cheating is not allowed.** Matches run on servers protected by Easy Anti-Cheat. Cheats, exploits, alternate accounts used to dodge a ban and any other unfair advantage end in a matchmaking ban, and a permanent one can come with a Steam game ban.
3. **Finish what you join.** Leaving a running match counts as an abandon. Repeated abandons put you on a cooldown and can keep you out of matchmaking.
4. **Respect other players.** Harassment, hate speech and deliberately ruining a match (griefing, feeding, idling) are punished.
5. Use the in-game **report** button when something is wrong. Reports go to the moderators together with what the anti-cheat recorded. False reports lower the weight of your future ones.
6. You can always see your own account on your profile here: your record, your quests and whether you can queue. If you think a ban is a mistake, contact the staff listed on the [staff page](/staff).
7. Moderators decide on bans case by case. A ban for the same behaviour is not guaranteed to have the same length.
